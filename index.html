Why it exists

Drives know a great deal and tell almost nobody.

Every drive in a plant holds hundreds of readings and a fault table in a manual nobody has to hand. Most monitoring products answer that with a cloud subscription, which many plants cannot connect to and many operators will not trust.

Fault at 02:40

A code on a screen is not an explanation

QuInt reads the vendor's own manual and answers "what is this alarm and what do I check" with the page it came from, in the control room or at the cabinet.

Hundreds of readings, one tiny display

Telemetry locked in the cabinet

The unit talks to the drive directly and shows every reading live, on a screen or a phone. No PLC change, no gateway licence, no integrator visit.

Air-gapped by policy

Cloud IoT needs a cloud

Many sites have no outbound connection by design. QuInt is standalone first: the dashboard, the model, the history and the approvals all live on the unit. A fleet server is added later, if ever.

How it works

Readings in, answers out, nothing leaves the site.

The unit polls the drive directly and serves everything on site. Commands to a drive are signed and short-lived, changes go through an approval gate, and every action is recorded.

Capabilities

Built for the people who stand next to the machine.

Standalone · on every unit
dashboard

Live view

Every configured reading, live, with trends and detail. A newly approved reading shows up on the next cycle.

assistant · manual

Answers with citations

Ask about a fault code and get the vendor's own answer, with the manual page it came from. What the manual does not cover is declined, not improvised.

assistant · deploy

Change by asking

Describe a new reading in plain language and the assistant drafts the change. It still goes through the same approval gate as the form.

approvals

Approval gate

Every change to a live drive has a requester, an approver and a record. Exemptions are recorded, never silent.

ops

Remote operations

Restart, reboot or shut down from a page. Commands are signed and expire quickly; power actions need a separate operator-held key, and never bypass the machine's own safety circuits.

ble

Phone beside the drive

Readings, alerts and the assistant on a phone over Bluetooth, standing at the cabinet. No Wi-Fi needed.

research

Two-person knowledge

New reference material for the assistant is proposed by one person and verified by another before it ships. Its provenance travels inside the document.

audit

Audit trail

Every recorded action, filterable and exportable. The export is itself audited.

history

History on the unit

Readings are kept on the unit itself, charted over any span, and exported as CSV. Raw and rolled-up retention are both settable.

Security posture

Designed for plants that cannot and will not use a cloud.

The security model is in signatures, approvals and the record, and every piece of it is tested.

Nothing leaves the siteTelemetry, manuals, the model and its answers stay on your network. There is no vendor cloud to breach, subscribe to, or lose.
Commands are signedCommands to a drive carry signatures and expire quickly. Power actions need a second, operator-held key.
Approvals are the ruleEvery change has a requester, an approver and a record. An exemption is itself a recorded decision, never a silent bypass.
Fail closedA unit that cannot verify a command refuses it with an error rather than acting on it.
Separate dutiesChanging what a drive measures and changing what the assistant knows are separate permissions. A site can trust different people with each.
Everything on the recordEvery action lands in an audit trail you can filter and export: who asked, who approved, what the device acknowledged.
